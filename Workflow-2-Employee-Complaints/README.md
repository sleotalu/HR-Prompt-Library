# Workflow 02 - Employee Complaints

**Business Function:** Human Resources (HR)\
**Trigger:** An employee submits a workplace complaint or raises a concern that requires HR review\
**Prompts in this section:** P05, P06, P07

## Section Purpose
This section supports HR in managing employee complaints through a structured and consistent process. It helps categorise employee concerns, review relevant workplace policies and prepare professional responses while maintaining confidentiality and fairness.

## Chain Diagram
```text
[Employee submits complaint]
          ↓
[P05 — Employee Complaint Categorisation]
          ↓
[Complaint Categorisation Report]
          ↓
[P06 — HR Policy Review]
          ↓
[HR Policy Review Findings]
          ↓
[P07 — Employee Complaint Response]
          ↓
[Draft Response to Employee]
```
