# Prompt 09: Employee Development Plan

## 1. Workflow

**Workflow Name:** Employee Performance Management

**Workflow Stage:** Employee Development Planning

**Previous Prompt:** Prompt 08 - Performance Feedback 

**Next Prompt:** Prompt 10 - Performance Review Summary

**Workflow Description:**
This prompt is used after employee performance feedback has been generated and reviewed. It helps HR staff and managers create structured employee development plans by identifying skill gaps, setting development goals and recommending suitable training and improvement activities.

## 2. Purpose
The purpose of this prompt is to assist HR staff and managers in developing practical and achievable employee development plans based on performance feedback and identified development needs. It aims to make employee development more efficient, structured and consistent by identifying areas for improvement, establishing clear development goals and recommending appropriate training and learning opportunities..

## 3. Prompt
``` text
You are an experienced HR employee development assistant responsible for helping managers create structured employee development plans to support workplace performance and professional growth.

Review the employee's performance feedback and development needs. Identify skill gaps and create a development plan with achievable goals, suitable activities, timeframes and measurable outcomes.

Use the following information:
- Company name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Employee name: [EMPLOYEE_NAME]
- Job title: [JOB_TITLE]
- Department: [DEPARTMENT]
- Manager name: [MANAGER_NAME]
- Performance feedback: [PERFORMANCE_FEEDBACK}
- Identified development needs: [DEVELOPMENT_NEEDS]
- Employee career goals: [CAREER_GOALS]
- Available training and resources: [AVAILABLE_RESOURCES]

Include the following sections:
1. Employee details and current performance summary
2. Identified skill gaps and improvement areas
3. Employee development goals
4. Recommended training and development activities
5. Suggested timeframes
6. Required resources and support
7. Measurable success indicators and expected outcomes
8. Progress monitoring and follow-up arrangements

Tone: supportive, constructive and professional

Output: Generate a structured employee development plan. using a table to organise goals, activities, timeframes and expected outcomes.

Do not include: invented skill gaps, unsupported assumptions or unrealistic development goals.
```

## 4. Intended Task
The intended task is to support the employee development planning stage of the employee performance management process. HR staff and managers can use this prompt to create individual development plans based on employee performance feedback, identified skill gaps and professional development needs. This helps employees understand what they need to improve, how they can develop their skills and what actions they can take to achieve their workplace and career goals.

## 5. Problem Being Solved
Creating individual employee development plans can be time-consuming, particularly when managers need to identify development needs, recommend suitable training and establish measurable goals. Without a structured approach, development plans may lack clear objectives, realistic timeframes or appropriate methods for measuring employee progress.
This prompt addresses these challenges by organising employee development needs into a structured plan, helping managers recommend relevant learning activities and establish clear expectations for employee improvement.

## 6. Automation Potential 
This prompt has the potential to automate parts of the employee development planning process. 

**Potential Automation Activities:**
- Review employee performance feedback and development needs
- Identify potential skill gaps from supplied information
- Generate employee development goals
- Recommend relevant training and learning activities
- Suggest development timeframes
- Establish measurable development outcomes
- Generate structured individual employee development plans
- Prepare draft development plans for manager and employee review

**Human Involvement:**
HR staff and managers must verify the development recommendations, ensure that the goals are realistic and relevant to the employee's role, and confirm that appropriate resources and training opportunities are available. Employees should be involved in discussing and agreeing on their development goals. AI should support development planning rather than independently determine employee career progression or make employment decisions. 

## 7. Risks and Limitations:**
**Potential Risks:**
- **Inappropriate Recommendations:** AI may recommend training or development activities that are not suitable for the employee's role or circumstances.
- **Incorrect Skills Assessment:** AI may incorectly identify skill gaps if the performance feedback is incomplete or inaccurate.
- **Unrealistic Goals:** AI may generate development goals or timeframes that are difficult to achieve.
- **Bias:** AI may produce unfair development recommendations based on incomplete or subjective performance information.
- **Privacy:** Employee development records may contain confidential personal and employment information.
- **Over-reliance:** Managers may depend too heavily on AI-generated development plans without considering employee preferences or individual circumstances.

**Limitations:**
- AI can only use the employee information and develop needs provided
- AI cannot independently assess an employee's actual skills or workplace performance
- AI may not have access to the organisation's available training programs or development resources
- AI cannot determine whether an employee has successfully completed a development activity
- Human judgement is required to approve development goals and assess employee progress

**Risk Mitigation:**
Provide accurate employee performance feedback, use current information about available training opportunities, protect confidential employee records and require HR staff and managers to review development recommendations before approving the final plan. Employees should be consulted to ensure that development goals are achievable and relevant to their needs.

## 8. Testing and Evaluation
The prompt will be tested using sample employee feedback, job responsibilities and identified development needs.

**Testing Method:**
1. Prepare a sample employee performance feedback report generated using Prompt 08
2. Identify the employee's strengths, improvement areas and development needs
3. Provide the employee's job responsibilities and relevant workplace expectations
4. Run the Employee Development Plan prompt
5. Review the generated employee development plan
6. Check whether the recommended goals and activities address the identifies development needs
7. Assess whether the suggested timeframes and success indicators are realistic and measurable
8. Identify unsuitable recommendations, missing information or unrealistic development goals
9. Refine the prompt where necessary and test again

**Evaluation Criteria:**
| Evaluation Area | What to Check |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Does the prompt correctly identify development needs from the supplied performance feedback? |
| Relevance | Are the recommended activities appropriate for the employee's role and responsibilities? |
| Consistency | Does it generate consistent development plans for employees with similar development needs? |
| Completeness | Does it include clear goals, activities, timeframes and measurable outcomes? |
| Feasibility | Are the development activities and timeframes realistic and achievable? |
| Output Quality | Is the development plan clear, organised and useful for HR staff, managers and employees? |

**Sucess Measures:**
The prompt should produce relevant, realistic and structured employee development plans while reducing the time managers spend identifying development needs and organising training activities.
