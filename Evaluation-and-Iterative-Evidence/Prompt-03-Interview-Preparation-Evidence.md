# Prompt 03: Interview Preparation

## 1. Overview
This document records the iterative development of Prompt 03: Interview Preparation. Three versions were developed to improve the structure, relevance and consistency of interview questions while supporting fair and professional recruitment practices.

## 4. Prompt Versions

### V1.0 - Initial Interview Preparation Prompt
``` text
You are an HR recruitment assistant responsible for supporting interview preparation.

Develop interview questions for the position of [JOB_TITLE] at [COMPANY_NAME].

Use the following information provided by HR:
- Job description: [JOB_DESCRIPTION}
- Candidate resume: [CANDIDATE_RESUME]

Review the job description and candidate resume to identify relevant areas to explore during the interview.

Requirements:
- Create relevant questions based on the job description
- Consider the candidate's qualifications, skills and experience
- Use clear and professional languag
- Ensure questions are appropriate for a workplace interview

Output: A list of interview questions to assist HR with interview preparation.
```

### V1.1 - Structured Interview Question Generator
``` text
You are an experienced HR recruitment assistant responsible for supporting professional and structured interview preparation.

Develop interview questions for the position of [JOB_TITLE] at [COMPANY_NAME].

Use the following information provided by HR:
- Job description: [JOB_DESCRIPTION]
- Candidate resume: [CANDIDATE_RESUME]
- Essential requirements: [ESSENTIAL REQUIREMENTS]
- Desirable criteria: [DESIRABLE_CRITERIA]
 Review the job description and candidate resume to develop relevant interview questions.

Include the following question categories:
1. Technical Questions: Assess the candidate's job-related skills and knowledge
2. Behavioural Questions: Explore the candidate's previous workplace experiences and actions
3. Situational Questions: Assess how the candidate would respond to realistic workplace scenarios

Requirements:
- Base questions on the job description and candidate information
- Ensure questions are relevant to the position
- Use clear, professional and appropriate language
- Avoid questions unrelated to the job
- Ensure questions support a fair and consistent interview process

Output: A structured list of technical, behavioural and situational questions for HR to review.
```

## 5. Summary of Improvements

### V1.0 to V1.1
- Introduced technical, behavioural and situational interview questions categories
- Included job description and candidate resume information
- Improved the variety and relevance of interview questions
- Established a more structured approach to interview preparation

### V1.1 to V1.2
- Introduced a structured interview guide with seven sections
- Incorporated candidate screening reports, strengths and areas requiring clarification
- Added follow-up questions and evaluation criteria
- Connected interview questions to output of Prompt 01 and Prompt 02
- Introduced safeguards against discriminatory or unrelated interview questions
- Established consistent assessment criteria for shortlisted candidates
- Confirmed that final hiring decisions remain the responsibility of HR

## 6, Final Outcome
V1.2 was selected as the final prompt because it provides a more comprehensive and structured approach to interview preparation. the final version uses the job description and candidate screening results to develop relevant interview questions, evaluation criteria and follow-up questions. It also supports fair and consistent interviews while ensuring that HR retains responsibility for evaluating candidates and making all final hiring decisions.
