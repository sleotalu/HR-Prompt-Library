# Prompt 07: Complaint Response

## 1. Overview
This document records the iterative development of Prompt 07: Complaint Response. Three versions were developed to improve the structure, relevance and consistency of responses to employee complaints. The improvements focus on acknowledging employee concerns, using complain categorisation and HR policy review findings, and providing appropriate information about complaint-handling processes while maintaining confidentiality and avoiding unsupported promises or conclusions.

## 2. Version History
| Version | Changes Made | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| V1.0 | Initial employee complaint response prompt created. | Establish the basic complaint response structure. |
| V1.1 | Added instructions to use complaint categorisation and HR policy review findings. | Improve relevance and consistency of responses. |
| V1.2 | Added confidentiality safeguards, communication requirements and mandatory HR approval. | Reduce communication risks and ensure appropriate human oversight. |

## 4. Prompt Versions

### V1.0 - Initial Employee Complaint Response Prompt
``` text
You are an HR assistant responsible for preparing professional and respectful responses to employee complaints.

Draft a response to an employee who has submitted a workplace complaint to [COMPANY_NAME].

Use the following information:
- Company name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Employee name: [EMPLOYEE_NAME]
- Employee complaint: [EMPLOYEE_COMPLAINT]
- Date complaint received: [DATE_RECEIVED]

Include the following sections:
1. Email subject line
2. Greeting
3. Acknowledgement of the employee's complaint
4. Brief response to the concerns raised
5. General next steps
6. Professional closing

Requirements:
- Acknowledge the employee's concerns respectfully.
- Use clear and professional language.
- Keep the response supportive and easy to understand.
- Do not invent complaint details or promise a particular outcome.

Tone: Professional, respectful and supportive.

Output: A professional employee complaint response in email format suitable for HR review.
```

### V1.1
``` text
You are an experienced HR assistant responsible for preparing professional, fair and respectful responses to employee complaints.

Review the employee complaint, complaint categorisation report and relevant HR policy review findings to draft an appropriate response.

Use the following information:
- Company name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Employee name: [EMPLOYEE_NAME]
- Employee complaint: [EMPLOYEE_COMPLAINT]
- Complaint categorisation report: [COMPLAINT_CATEGORISATION_REPORT]
- HR policy review findings: [HR_POLICY_REVIEW_FINDINGS]
- Next steps identified by HR: [HR_NEXT_STEPS]
- HR contact name: [HR_CONTACT_NAME]

Include the following sections:
1. Email subject line
2. Greeting
3. Acknowledgement of complaint
4. Summary of the concerns raised
5. Explanation of the complaint-handling process
6. Relevant next steps identified by HR
7. Professional closing

Requirements:
- Use the complaint categorisation report and HR policy review findings to guide the response.
- Acknowledge the employee's concerns respectfully.
- Explain the relevant next steps using the information provided by HR.
- Use clear, supportive and professional language.
- Do not make assumptions or promise a particular complaint outcome.
- Do not disclose confidential information about other employees.

Tone: Supportive, respectful and professional.

Output: A structured employee complaint response in email format suitable for HR review.
```

## 5. Summary of Improvements

### V1.0 to V1.1
- Introduced the complaint categorisation report generated by Prompt 05
- Added HR policy review findings from prompt 06 to guide the response
- Introduced a clearer complaint response structure
- Added an explanation of the complaint-handling process
- Included next steps identified by HR
- Improved the relevance and consistency of responses by connecting the prompt to previous workflow outputs

### V1.1 to V1.2
- Added employe email address and department details
- Included the date the complaint was received and the original employee complaint
- Added additional information requests where clarification or documentation is required
- Introduced available employee support information
- Expanded the response structure to include relevant contact details and complaint-handling information
- Added safeguards against assumptions, unsupported statements and promises about complaint outcomes or unconfirmed timeframes
- Introduced restrictions on disclosing confidential information about other employees
- Improved the supportive, respectful and understanding tone of employee communications
- Improved response accuracy, consistency and confidentiality while maintaining HR oversight

## 6. Final Outcome
V1.2 was selected as the final prompt because it provides a more complete and structured approach to responding to employee complaints. The final version uses the employee complaint, complaint categorisation report from Prompt 05 and HR policy review findings from Prompt 06 to prepare an appropriate response. It supports HR by acknowledging employee concerns, explaining the complaint-handling process and communicating relevant next steps and any additional information required.

The final prompt also reduces the risk of misleading or inappropriate communications by preventing unsupported assumptions, promised about complaint outcomes, unconfirmed timeframes and disclosure of confidential employee information. All generated responses remain subject to HR to review before being sent to employees.
