# Prompt 06: HR Policy Review

## 1. Overview
This document records the iterative development of Prompt 06: HR Policy Review. Three versions were developed to improve the structure, relevance and consistency of workplace policy reviews. The improvements focus on comparing employee complaints against supplied organisational policies, identifying relevant policy requirements and highlighting areas requiring further HR assessment while avoiding unsupported conclusions.

## 2. Version History
| Version | Changes Made | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| V1.0 | Initial HR policy review prompt created. | Establish the basic policy review structure. |
| V1.1 | Added instructions to compare complaints against supplied policies and identify relevant requirements. | Improve policy relevance and assessment consistency. |
| V1.2 | Added safeguards against unsupported conclusions, outdated information and privacy risks. | Improve reliability and ensure appropriate HR oversight. |

## 4. Prompt Versions

### V1.0 - Initial HR Policy Review Report
``` text
You are an HR assistant responsible for reviewing workplace complaints against company policies.

Review an employee complaint and identify any relevant workplace policies or procedures that may relate to the issues raised.

Use the following information:
- Company name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Employee name: [EMPLOYEE_NAME]
- Employee complaint: [EMPLOYEE_COMPLAINT]
- Relevant HR policies: [HR_POLICIES]

Include the following sections:
1. Summary of the complaint
2. Main workplace issues identified
3. Relevant policies or procedures
4. Suggested next steps for HR review

Requirements:
- Use the information provided
- Identify policies that may relate to the complaint
- Do not invent policy information
- Keep the review clear and easy to understand

Tone: Professional and objective

Output: A structured HR policy review summary to help HR understand the complaint and relevant workplace policies.
```

### V1.1 - Structured HR Policy Review Prompt
``` text
You are an experienced HR policy assistant responsible for supporting the review of workplace complaints against organisational policies and procedures. 

Review an employee complaint and compare the issues raised against the relevant HR policies and procedures supplied by [COMPANY_NAME].

Use the following information:
- Company name: [COMPANY_NAME]
- Employee name: [EMPLOYEE_NAME]
- Employee complaint: [EMPLOYEE_COMPLAINT]
- Relevant HR policies and procedures: [HR_POLICIES_AND_PROCEDURES]
- Supporting documents or evidence: [SUPPORTING_DOCUMENTS]

Include the following sections:
1. Summary of the employee complaint
2. Main workplace issues identified
3. Relevant HR policies and procedures
4. Specific policy requirements that may apply
5. How the complaint relates to the identified policies
6. Potential policy concerns requiring further review
7. Suggested next steps for HR staff

Requirements:
- Compare the complaint against the policies and procedures provided
- Identify relevant policy requirements based on the supplied information
- Use only the information provided
- Do not invent policy requirements or make assumptions
- Do not make final conclusions about policy breaches or employee misconduct
- Identify any areas requiring further HR review

Tone: Neutral, objective and professional

Output: A structured HR policy review report support consistent and informed HR assessment.
```

## 5. Summary of Improvements

### V1.0 to V1.1
- Introduced instructions to compare employee complaints against supplied HR policies and procedures
- Added relevant policy requirements that may apply to the issues raised
- Introduced a structured comparison between complaint issues and workplace policies
- Added supporting documents or evidence as information for the review
- Improved consistency when identifying potential policy concerns
- Added instructions to avoid assumptions and unsupported policy information

### V1.1 to V1.2
- Connected the policy review process to the employee complaint categorisation report generated by Prompt 05
- Added employee department and complaint receipt date to the information provided
- Expanded the report to include specific policy requirements and explanations of how the complaint relates to identified policies
- Introduced missing information and supporting evidence checks
- Added potential areas of non-compliance or concern requiring further review
- Added recommendations for appropriate HR follow-up
- Introduced safeguards against invented policy requirements, legal conclusions and final determinations of policy breaches
- Clarified that the prompt must not conclude that an employee is responsible for misconduct
- Improved the accuracy, consistency and reliability of HR policy reviews

## 6. Final Outcome
V1.2 was selected as the final prompt because it provides a more complete and structured approach to reviewing employee complaints against organisational policies and procedures. The final version uses the employee complaint categorisation report from Prompt 05, together with relevant HR policies and supporting evident, to identify applicable policy requirements and potential areas of concern. It also highlights missing information and recommends appropriate HR follow-up.

The prompt reduces the risk of inaccurate policy assessments by requiring the use of supplied organisational policies and preventing invented requirements, unsupported assumptions or final conclusions about policy breaches. HR staff remain responsible for reviewing the findings and making any final determinations.
